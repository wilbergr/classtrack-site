Made to make schoolwork feel lighter

Everything in ClassTrack is built to encourage you — nothing is ever taken away, and nothing ever shames you for a busy week.

🔥

A sidekick that grows with you

Pick a companion and watch it evolve as you complete work. It only ever moves forward — it never regresses, gets sick, or loses a form you've earned.

🛡️

Momentum & shields

A forgiving take on streaks: shields protect your momentum on busy days instead of punishing you for missing one. Life happens — your progress stays.

✨

Earn Sparks

Finishing work earns Sparks, ClassTrack's in-app reward. Spend them in a shop of fun cosmetic looks for your app and companion.

📚

Everything in one place

Organize homework, tests, and projects by subject, each with its own color. One glance at Today shows what's due now and what's coming up.

🔔

Gentle reminders

A nudge the evening before and the morning something's due — scheduled entirely on your phone, never pushy, and overdue work simply waits for whenever you're ready.

🔒

Private by design

No account, no backend, no tracking. ClassTrack can't share your data with anyone because it never collects any in the first place.
