Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. ClassTrack has no accounts at all — just open the app and start adding subjects and assignments. Everything is stored on your device.

What happens if I miss a day?

Nothing bad, ever. Shields protect your momentum on busy days, your sidekick never regresses, and overdue work simply waits for whenever you're ready.

Does ClassTrack work offline?

Yes, completely. There's no server behind ClassTrack, so everything — assignments, reminders, your companion's progress — works without any connection.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

ClassTrack never uploads your data anywhere, so it travels with your phone's own backup and device-transfer tools. A built-in export is on our wish list — tell us if you'd like it sooner!

When is ClassTrack coming to the app stores?

Soon! It's in its final stretch of testing on iPhone and Android. The download links on the home page will go live the moment it ships.
