Privacy Policy

The short version: ClassTrack collects nothing. Here's the whole policy.

No data collection

ClassTrack does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All assignment, subject, and progress data is stored locally on your device only — there is no account, no server, and no analytics.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled entirely on-device using your phone's operating system; no notification data is sent to any external server.

Voice input

Optional voice input, if enabled in Settings, uses your device's built-in speech recognition. Depending on your device and OS settings, this may be processed by Apple or Google's on-device or cloud speech services — the same as any other app using your phone's dictation feature. ClassTrack itself does not receive, store, or transmit this audio or its transcription.

Ads and third parties

ClassTrack contains no ads and does not share data with third parties, because it collects none.
